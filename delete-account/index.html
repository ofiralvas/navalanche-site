Delete your Navalanche account

You can delete your Navalanche account and its data at any time, for free. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Navalanche.
  2. Go to Settings, then the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete my account.
  4. Read the list of what gets deleted, type DELETE, then tap Delete my account. (Changed your mind? Tap Keep my account.)

Your data is deleted immediately and you'll see "Your account is deleted". The next time you open the app, you start as a brand-new rider.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

  1. Email ofir.alvas@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Navalanche account".
  2. Include your username and, if you can, your Account ID (Settings → Account → Account ID, with a Copy button). Navalanche accounts are anonymous, so this is how we find yours. The name of a friend you rode with also helps.
  3. We'll confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What gets deleted

  • Your account and anonymous ID
  • Your profile: username, bio, skill level, gear, profile photo
  • Your last known location
  • Messages, voice messages and waves you sent
  • Your friend list, friend requests and blocks
  • Groups you created, and your membership in other groups
  • Your notification (push) tokens

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Reports you made about other riders are kept without your account ID for up to 12 months, for safety.
  • Crash reports expire automatically within 90 days, and basic usage events within 12 months. Neither contains your location, and once your account is deleted they can no longer be connected to a profile.
  • Data saved only on your phone (ride stats, settings) is removed when you delete the account or uninstall the app.

Just want to stop sharing your location?

You don't have to delete your account. Turn on Ghost mode in the app and your location is removed from our server and hidden from friends until you turn it off. You can also turn off location access in your phone's settings.

Note: uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Please use Option 1 before uninstalling.
